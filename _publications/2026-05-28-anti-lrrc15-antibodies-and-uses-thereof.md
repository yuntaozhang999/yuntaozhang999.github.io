This patent publication discloses isolated monoclonal antibodies and antigen-binding fragments that specifically target human Leucine-Rich Repeat Containing 15 (LRRC15). The disclosed antibodies exhibit binding affinity, selectivity, and cellular internalization upon engaging LRRC15 expressed on tumor cells and cancer-associated fibroblasts (CAFs).

The patent encompasses multiple molecular formats including full-length antibodies, bispecific/multispecific antibodies, and immunoconjugates, along with methods for diagnosing, prognosing, and treating solid tumors characterized by stromal desmoplasia and LRRC15 expression.
